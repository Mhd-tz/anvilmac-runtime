## What's new

- **A newer DXMT** (the Direct3D 10 and 11 translator): DXMT's main branch at 647b002, 384 changes after v0.80,
  with many fixes and more of Direct3D 11 covered.
- **Correct screen size for games in Retina mode**: with Retina mode on, Windows programs were told the screen was
  twice its real size, so some games placed their window off screen (Wine patch 0019, from athei/wine).
- **A rare startup problem fixed**: a timing race could leave a game's window drawing but never receiving keyboard
  or mouse input (Wine patch 0020, from athei/wine).
- Everything from 11.19-15, including x87sidecar for older 32-bit games.

## Measured

- Void's Cave and Mothville (Direct3D 11): at their 60 fps cap, as on 11.19-15, with no DXMT errors.
- Irreplaceable Turbines (Direct3D 11): the same frame rate as on 11.19-15.
- A hidden Direct3D 11 test: about 120 fps, as on 11.19-15.

## Known problems

- Unigine Heaven's Direct3D 11 mode shows a black window (as on 11.19-15); its Direct3D 9 mode works.
- Bink videos in the ReXGlue ports (Naruto) stay slow. That is an issue in the ports and has been reported to them.
- x87sidecar does x87 math with 64-bit instead of 80-bit precision. If a 32-bit game behaves differently, turn
  "Faster math for older 32-bit games" off in its Game Settings.
