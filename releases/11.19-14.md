## What's new

- **Frame rate for Direct3D 10 and 11 games**: games that run through DXMT now report how fast they draw. AnvilMac
  shows it on the game's Sessions tab (typical frame rate and the slowest moments), as it already did for other games.
- Everything from 11.19-13, including msync.
- The first runtime release with a signed manifest: AnvilMac checks the signature before it installs a runtime from
  GitHub.

## Measured

- A hidden Direct3D 11 test on an M2 Pro (32- and 64-bit) reports about 118 to 120 frames per second, matching its own
  count; with the frame-rate channel off it writes nothing.
- msync unchanged: event set 7.7 to 0.37 microseconds; five pairs of threads handing off 58.8 to 11.4 microseconds.
- Played on this runtime by the owner: Mothville (frame rate measured: 60 fps, the cap).

## Known problems

- Bink videos in the ReXGlue ports (Naruto) stay slow. That is an issue in the ports and has been reported to them.
