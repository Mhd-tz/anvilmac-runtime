## What's new

- **Game sound follows your Mac's output**: when AirPods connect, or you pick another output in Control Center or in
  AnvilMac's Settings › Sound, a running game's sound moves with it. Before, it stayed on the old device until the
  game restarted (Wine patch 0022).
- **Steadier sound and timing in games that pace themselves with short waits**: on macOS a short Sleep woke about a
  quarter late, and a yield could stall a thread for 10 ms. Games that make their sound in small timed slices could
  fall behind and crackle; Sonic Unleashed Recompiled's sound broke after about 28 minutes of play. Short waits now
  wake on time (Wine patch 0021; `WINEPRECISESLEEP=0` turns it off).
- **OpenGL games that use GLFW start again**: Wine linked their OpenGL context to one of the wrong kind, which macOS
  refuses, so games such as Project Zomboid quit after a few seconds without a window (Wine patch 0023, from WineHQ).
- **Correct sound timestamps on Macs that haven't restarted for a day**: Wine's audio position timestamps overflowed
  after about 25.6 hours of uptime (sleep included), so games that use them got wrong values (Wine patch 0024, from
  WineHQ). Two small Mac fixes from WineHQ too: a window style value that could be random (0025) and memory kept
  when a game window's Metal layer is set up (0026).
- Everything from 11.19-16, including the newer DXMT and x87sidecar for older 32-bit games.

## Measured

- Sonic Unleashed Recompiled, on a local build with the same sleep patch: 44 minutes of play with no audible problem
  (before: crackling from about 28 minutes on until the game was closed). Its audio thread woke more than 2 ms late 4 times in 499,000 waits.
- A test of the same audio loop pattern: 0 of 900 slices lost on an idle Mac (23 before), 31 with every core busy
  (237 before).
- Project Zomboid 42.22 (OpenGL): starts and plays, median 75 fps (8 minutes on this build, some drops).
- Sonic Unleashed Recompiled on this build: 32 minutes at 60 fps, sound fine.
- All 12 release checks pass; DirectX 11 frame rate unchanged (about 120 fps in the test).

## Known problems

- Sonic Unleashed Recompiled: one stretch of about a minute with some missing sound was measured in that 44-minute
  session (not heard). It came from a different part of the sound path; not yet explained.
- Unigine Heaven's Direct3D 11 mode shows a black window (as on 11.19-16); its Direct3D 9 mode works.
- Naruto: Rise of a Ninja (ReXGlue port 0.4.2): under Wine its default renderer is forced onto Vulkan (MoltenVK on a
  Mac), which makes cutscenes and gameplay slow. Its Xenos renderer (`renderer = 'xenos'` in its `data/narutorise.toml`)
  draws through Direct3D 12 on D3DMetal instead; AnvilMac sets it by itself when Apple's Game Porting Toolkit is imported.
- x87sidecar does x87 math with 64-bit instead of 80-bit precision. If a 32-bit game behaves differently, turn
  "Faster math for older 32-bit games" off in its Game Settings.
