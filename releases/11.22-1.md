## What's new

- **Built on Wine 11.22** (was 11.19): three Wine development releases of fixes from WineHQ, among them Tahoma Bold
  that is bold again, CD drives handled by Wine's Mount Manager, better user-account support, XML validation reworked,
  locale data from Unicode CLDR 49, and address space randomization.
- **Five of AnvilMac's Wine fixes are now part of Wine itself** (correct screen size on Retina displays, OpenGL games
  that use GLFW such as Project Zomboid, sound timestamps after a day of uptime, and two small Mac fixes), so this
  runtime carries fewer changes of its own. Everything else from 11.19-17 is kept: game sound that follows your Mac's
  output, short waits on time, msync, x87sidecar for older 32-bit games, DXMT and D3DMetal.
- **Games installed from a PC disc or a Windows installer can move to it**: AnvilMac copies their Windows environment
  and lets Wine 11.22 update the copy; the old one stays, so they can go back. This needs an AnvilMac that has this
  runtime built in; earlier versions keep such games on their current runtime.

## Measured

- The owner played on this build: Sonic Unleashed Recompiled (Direct3D 12 on D3DMetal), SPORE (32-bit, with
  x87sidecar), Warcraft III (OpenGL), Max Payne and The Sims 3; all fine. Warcraft III, Max Payne and The Sims 3 are
  installed games and moved from 11.19-17.
- All 12 release checks pass. DirectX 11 frame rate unchanged (about 120 fps in the test); x87sidecar 29 times faster
  with identical results; msync wakes a waiting thread in 0.38 µs (7.32 µs without it).

## Known problems

- In the 32-bit transition test, one of 30 runs crashed inside macOS's own code (0 of 20 on 11.19-17). Rare and not
  shown to be new in this runtime; tell us if a 32-bit game quits at random.
- Not rechecked on this runtime, as on 11.19-17: Unigine Heaven's Direct3D 11 mode shows a black window (its Direct3D 9
  mode works); Sonic Unleashed Recompiled had one stretch of about a minute with some missing sound in a 44-minute
  measurement (not heard).
- Naruto: Rise of a Ninja (ReXGlue port 0.4.2): under Wine its default renderer is forced onto Vulkan (MoltenVK on a
  Mac), which makes cutscenes and gameplay slow. Its Xenos renderer (`renderer = 'xenos'` in its `data/narutorise.toml`)
  draws through Direct3D 12 on D3DMetal instead; AnvilMac sets it by itself when Apple's Game Porting Toolkit is imported.
- x87sidecar does x87 math with 64-bit instead of 80-bit precision. If a 32-bit game behaves differently, turn
  "Faster math for older 32-bit games" off in its Game Settings.
